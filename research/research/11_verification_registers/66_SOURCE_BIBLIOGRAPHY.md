# SIH 26121 — Comprehensive Source Bibliography

## 1. Executive Summary
This document provides a formal, structured bibliography of foundational petroleum engineering textbooks, peer-reviewed SPE conference papers, machine learning publications, regulatory standards, and industry technical specifications underpinning the NWIS research corpus.
