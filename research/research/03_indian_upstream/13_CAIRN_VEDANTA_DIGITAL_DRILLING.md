# SIH 26121 — Cairn Oil & Gas (Vedanta): Digital Drilling & Data Ecosystem

## 1. Executive Summary
Cairn Oil & Gas, a vertical of Vedanta Limited, is India's largest private upstream exploration and production company, accounting for roughly a quarter of India's domestic crude production. Operating predominantly in the desert basins of Barmer (Rajasthan), Cambay (Gujarat), and the offshore Ravva field (KG Basin), Cairn has pioneered aggressive digital transformation through **Project Nirmaan**, its **PetroTechnical Cloud**, and an enterprise alliance with **SLB and Cognite**.

---

## 2. Operating Environment & Scale
- **Flagship Asset:** Rajasthan Block RJ-ON-90/1 (Barmer Basin), containing the Mangala, Bhagyam, and Aishwarya (MBA) fields.
- **Drilling Context:** Massive infill drilling programs, polymer flooding, enhanced oil recovery (EOR), tight gas hydraulic fracturing, and horizontal drilling. Over 1,000+ wells drilled in the Barmer basin over the past two decades.
- **Operational Need:** With hundreds of closely spaced infill wells drilled across intersecting reservoirs, historical offset well analysis is critical to avoid well collisions, manage reservoir depletion pressure drops, and optimize drilling rates.

## 3. Project Nirmaan: Operational Document Analytics
- **Program Identity:** *Project Nirmaan* is Cairn's flagship enterprise operational transformation initiative.
- **Analytics Scope:** Involves extensive text mining, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and predictive analytics across large repositories of operational records:
  - Daily operational logs, maintenance records, and equipment failure reports.
  - Identification of recurring operational bottlenecks and Non-Productive Time (NPT) root causes.
  - Automated structuring of historical field knowledge for engineering reuse.

## 4. PetroTechnical Cloud & Real-Time Data Integration
Cairn was an early mover in India in deploying a cloud-hosted **PetroTechnical Platform**:
- **Data Integration:** Unifies real-time drilling telemetry, well logging data (LWD/MWD), reservoir simulation models, and surface production SCADA into a single collaborative cloud environment.
- **Real-Time Surveillance:** Rig-site sensors stream operational parameters to a centralized surveillance center in Gurgaon and regional offices in Barmer.
- **Cross-Disciplinary Workflows:** Subsurface geologists and real-time drilling engineers collaborate on live geosteering decisions using synchronized geological models and active well telemetry.

## 5. The SLB + Cognite Enterprise Alliance at Cairn
- **Strategic Partnership:** Cairn formally engaged the global alliance between **SLB (Schlumberger)** and **Cognite** to accelerate its data digitalization.
- **Technology Deployed:** Implementation of **Cognite Data Fusion (CDF)** contextualization platform across Cairn's subsurface and operational assets.
- **Relevance to PS 26121:**
  - Cognite Data Fusion automatically contextualizes and links structured sensor data (time-series) with unstructured documents (PDFs, schematics, operational records) using an underlying industrial knowledge graph.
  - Demonstrates that the concept of combining real-time telemetry with historical document contextualization is actively deployed in the Indian upstream private sector.

## 6. Strategic Takeaways for NWIS
1. **Proven Commercial Value in India:** Cairn's adoption of Cognite and NLP text mining proves that unstructured operational document intelligence delivers measurable economic value in Indian drilling operations.
2. **Distinct Architectural Boundary:** While Cairn achieved this by procuring high-cost international enterprise software (Cognite/SLB), Oil India Limited's PS 26121 seeks an agile, purpose-built, sovereign decision-support platform designed specifically to integrate with OIL's in-house eRTMAC system.
