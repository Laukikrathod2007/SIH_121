# SIH 26121 — Oil India Limited: Public Evidence Register

## 1. Executive Summary
This register documents all primary, official, and audited public evidence directly pertaining to Oil India Limited (OIL). Every factual assertion regarding OIL's operational scale, eRTMAC capabilities, software systems, and data infrastructure is cross-referenced against an explicit primary document ID in this register.
