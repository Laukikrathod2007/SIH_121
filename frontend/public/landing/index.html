01 · Historical Drilling Intelligence

From legacy scanned PDFs to
structured WITSML 2.0

When OIL data clerks dump 500 scanned PDFs of old Daily Drilling Reports (DDRs) and Well Completion Reports (WCRs) from the 1990s into a shared folder, NWIS automatically runs a background layout-aware parser — eliminating weeks of tedious manual entry.

A
Layout-Aware Vision OCR

Tesseract + EasyOCR with LayoutParser detects table boundaries, handwritten notes, and mud logs across multi-page scans.

B
Domain NER & Operational Extraction

Extracts formation tops, mud weights, casing depths, and operational commentary (e.g. “Total fluid loss of 40 bbl at 3,248 m; mixed 25 bbl nut-plug pill”).

C
Automated NPT & Cost Calculation

Quantifies exact Non-Productive Time hours lost and estimates financial impact — saved into structured WITSML 2.0 records.

02 · Geospatial Offset Well Intelligence

Nearby ≠ Relevant.
Subsurface-aware basin correlation.

The map does not simply plot geographical distance. It evaluates whether offset wells share the same geological fault block and formation. Wells across sealing fault barriers with divergent pressure regimes are isolated, while wells along continuous sandstone strike are highlighted in bright cyan.

01
Upper Assam Basin Coverage

Nahorkatiya, Moran, Digboi, and Baghjan wells mapped in geospatial coordinate space.

02
Fault-Block Barrier Awareness

Automatically excludes wells separated by major sealing faults to prevent misleading pore pressure analogies.

03
AHP Multi-Criteria Analogy Score

6-criterion Analytic Hierarchy Process scores formation match (87%), pressure regime (91%), and lithology.

04 · Geological & Stratigraphic Correlation

See exactly what is
ahead of the drill bit

NWIS automatically correlates formation tops from offset wells against the proposed trajectory — aligned to True Vertical Depth Subsea (TVDSS). Formation uncertainty bands (±35–120 m) and historical incident depths are mapped side-by-side.

01
TVDSS Horizon Alignment

Corrects for wellbore inclination (e.g. 35° deviated trajectories) to compare true formation thicknesses across wells.

02
Historical Incident Overlays

Every stuck-pipe, kick, and fluid loss recorded in legacy reports is plotted at its exact geological horizon.

05 · Predictive Drilling Risk Analytics

Quantify every risk
before it manifests downhole

NWIS continuously executes a physics + ML ensemble model that outputs probability scores for 5 core hazard families. Instead of a single opaque number, it exposes decoupled confidence pillars so engineers understand the physical basis.

•
5 Core Hazard Families

Lost circulation risk (ΔQ flow out), stuck pipe (overpull & MSE), kick/overpressure (d-exponent), torque & drag stick-slip, and cement bond integrity.

•
Physics + Machine Learning Ensemble

Combines deterministic hydraulics equations with XGBoost and LSTM classifiers trained on 30 years of NHK incidents.

06 · Real-Time Proactive Alert & Lookahead System

35 to 45 minutes of lead time
before entering the fracture zone

Traditional drilling monitors trigger alarms after mud levels drop and circulation is lost. NWIS looks ahead of the bit, alerting tool-pushers and company men while there is still time to adjust parameters and avoid NPT.

SAFE
Green Band (>50 m ahead)

Parameters within normal operating envelope. Routine logging active.

WARN
Amber Band (15–50 m ahead)

Proactive intervention recommended with exact recipe formulation and stand countdown.

CRIT
Red Band (<15 m ahead)

Imminent hazard detection. Automatic notification sent to town RTOC and rig superintendent.
