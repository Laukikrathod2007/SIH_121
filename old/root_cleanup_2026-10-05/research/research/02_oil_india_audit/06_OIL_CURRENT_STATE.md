## 4. Subsurface Geological Challenges in OIL Operational Areas

Drilling in Upper Assam is widely recognized as one of the most operationally challenging environments in India due to distinct stratigraphic hazards:

| Stratigraphic Group / Formation | Typical Depth Range (MD) | Primary Lithology | Core Drilling Hazards Encountered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alluvium & Dihing** | 0 – 600 m | Unconsolidated sands, gravels, clays | Surface washouts, gravel pack-offs, shallow aquifer influx. |
| **Tipam Sandstone** | 1,200 – 2,400 m | Coarse permeable sandstones interbedded with clay | Massive mud losses (depleted sands), differential sticking, bit balling in wet clays. |
| **Surma Group** | 2,200 – 2,800 m | Shales, siltstones, sandstones | High pore pressure ramps, tight hole on connections, swelling shales. |
| **Barail Group (Coal-Shale)** | 2,800 – 3,600 m | Alternating sandstones, carbonaceous shales, thick coal beds | Severe borehole enlargement, sloughing coal seams, mechanical stuck pipe, gas kicks from coal stringers. |
| **Kopili Formation** | 3,400 – 4,000 m | Micro-fractured, highly reactive marine shales | Chemical and mechanical wellbore instability, tight hole, pack-offs, stuck BHA requiring sidetracks. |
| **Sylhet Limestone & Eocene** | 3,800 – 4,800 m | Dense carbonates, fractured dolomites | Severe cavernous lost circulation, sudden total loss of returns, overpressured gas caps. |

## 6. Audit Verdict on Operator Technical Maturity

**Myth:** Oil India Limited is an analog company with paper binders and no digital infrastructure.  
**Reality:** `[CONTRADICTED]`. OIL operates modern real-time surveillance (eRTMAC), utilizes digital well engineering tools (Landmark Well Plan), and executes multi-rig remote data transmission.  
**The True Vulnerability:** OIL's digital systems are **siloed by phase**. Live data streams to eRTMAC, but historical well completion reports, daily tour sheets, and legacy event logs are sequestered in static PDF archives. When an active rig encounters an anomalous pressure or torque trend, the connection to relevant historical offset precedent depends on individual memory rather than an automated, contextual intelligence platform.
